They Smash Kings Too (Şahları da Vururlar)

They Smash Kings Too is a fast-paced puzzle game where chess movement meets heavy impact. Drop pieces onto an 8x8 board, line up attack paths, and smash every King to clear each stage.

Core Gameplay

Place and Smash: Pick from three pieces per turn and place them on open squares to strike along classic chess paths.

Clear the Kings: Every stage centers on taking down the Kings on the board. Line up angles, chain hits, and clear targets before running out of moves.

Simple to Pick Up: Easy for anyone to grasp in seconds, with deeper tactical angles as layouts tighten.
